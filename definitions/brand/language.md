The words. Getting these wrong is the fastest way to sound like everyone else.

## Names

DW-OS is the product. Digital Workers is the company. Never "the DW-OS
platform", never "DWOS", never "dw-os" in prose.

The console is for whoever operates the system. The dashboard is for whoever
runs the business. They are different surfaces and the words are not
interchangeable.

A connector connects to one tool. A definition file is a definition file, not
a config. A finding is what a rule flagged. A briefing is what a model wrote
for one role.

Say tools, not integrations. Say records, not rows, when a person is reading.
Say numbers, not metrics, outside the docs.

## Phrases we never use

"single source of truth" as a promise. We say what it does: one place where
the company agreed what a customer is.

"AI-powered" as an adjective on anything. Say which part uses a model and what
it is handed.

"real-time". Sync runs on a schedule and we say so.

"enterprise-grade". We sell to companies of 5 to 50 people.

"effortless", "in minutes", "no setup required". Setup is work. See
`objections.md`.

## How to say the hard things

When a number can't be measured, say which one and why. A briefing that names
a broken measurement is worth more than one that narrates around it.

When something is off by default, say it is off by default.

When we don't know, say we don't know.
