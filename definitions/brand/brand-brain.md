## What we are

Digital Workers builds DW-OS: an AI operating system a company owns as code.
It connects the tools a business already pays for, keeps every record exactly
as it arrived, rebuilds the whole picture from that, and answers questions
with numbers that can show their receipts.

The code is open source under AGPL-3.0 at github.com/digital-workers-ai/os.
Running it on a company's real tools is work we do with them.

## Who it is for

A company of 5 to 50 people running a dozen tools, where nobody's job title
says data and the founder is the fallback analyst. See `audiences.md`.

## The problem, in one paragraph

Sales has one Acme, billing has another, support has a third. Nobody can say
which revenue figure is the real one. A big company hires a data team. A small
company can't, so it lives with the mess or buys a dashboard that hides it.

## What makes us different

Three things. Only these three.

1. Every number traces to the tool it came from, the records it counted, and
   the records that were missing the field.
2. The business logic is plain files the company owns and versions. When a
   number moves, you can tell whether the business moved or the definition did.
3. An AI engineer reviews the system nightly and writes up fixes with evidence.
   A person approves before anything is built.

## What we never claim

- No number without a source. Every figure in an asset comes from `proof.md`.
- No AI that decides. A person approves before anything changes.
- No setup with no work in it. Connecting a company's real tools takes a
  conversation, and we say so.
- No competitor named in an ad we didn't measure. Counts come from what the
  public libraries actually showed.

## How an asset gets made

A slot on the calendar, or something a competitor did, becomes a draft. A
person approves the draft. Then it gets built. Every claim in it points at a
line in `proof.md` or at a record in the estate. If it points at nothing, it
doesn't ship.
