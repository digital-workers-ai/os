# quote-card

One sentence somebody actually said or wrote, and who said it. The
quote is verbatim: from proof.md, a README passage, or a transcript the
skill read. The who names the source, never a private person. An
opening quotation mark in the accent colour is the only decoration. The
picture, when there is one, fills the outer frame under the paper card.

Square and portrait: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16
