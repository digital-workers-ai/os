# stat-card

One number, what it counts, and one line on why it matters. The number
is the card: a reader sees it before reading anything else. The stat
comes from proof.md exactly as listed, the label says what it counts,
and the support says what that means for the reader. The picture, when
there is one, fills the outer frame under the paper card.
