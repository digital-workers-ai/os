# carousel

A cover, three to ten slides, and a closing, each rendered as its own
image. The cover names the subject and carries a kicker that says what
the reader gets. Every slide makes one claim: a short title and a body
that says it in full, with the fact from proof.md the claim rests on.
The closing is the only slide that asks for anything. Every page shows
its place in the set. The picture, when there is one, fills the outer
frame under the paper card of the page it belongs to.
