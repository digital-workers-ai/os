# list-card

A title and exactly three items. Three because a reader keeps three; a
list of four is two posts. The items are parallel: same shape, same
length, each one a fact from proof.md or a step the reader can take.
Numbers in the accent colour count the items. The picture, when there
is one, fills the outer frame under the paper card.

Square and portrait: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16
